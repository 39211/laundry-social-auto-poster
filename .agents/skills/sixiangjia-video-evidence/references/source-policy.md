# Source policy

## Accepted target

Count accepted, atomic, deduplicated, traceable evidence units—not URLs, search results, reposts, or AI summaries.

- A: 1500 historical/core units across ten domains.
- B: 500 implementation/case units, reviewed at 150, 350, and 500 milestones.
- C: 500 frontier research units from papers, official technical reports, reproducible repositories/releases, or evidenced cases.

Domains: promotional strategy and psychology; screenplay/dramaturgy; directing/performance; camera/lighting; edit/TTS/subtitles/audio; Grok/video workflow; realism/continuity/artifacts; garment care; shoe/bag/leather care; Meta/IG publishing and measurement.

Screenplay evidence must address character, situation, desire, obstacle, conflict, emotional turn, scene action, dialogue, resolution, or dramatic structure. Advertising copy, voiceover text, prompts, shot lists, storyboards, and shooting scripts do not count as screenplay evidence unless the claim is specifically about their later adaptation from an already-defined screenplay.

## Tiers

- T0: private Sixiangjia evidence, consented assets, confirmed SOP, real outcomes.
- T1: law, standards, official platform/model documentation, peer-reviewed research.
- T2: official courses, reproducible repositories, method-and-data industry studies.
- T3: identifiable creator with complete demonstration or case evidence.
- T4: X/forums/community hypotheses; discovery only.
- T5: AI summaries; search leads only and never accepted evidence.

Only T0–T2 may create hard rules. T3–T4 need corroboration and explicit limitations.

## Deduplication

Normalize URLs and canonical IDs; hash content; group translations, mirrors, reposts, and paraphrases. Keep one primary record and attach corroboration links. Preserve superseded sources but exclude them from current rules.

Do not split one source paragraph into many units solely to inflate counts. One unit must contain one independently actionable claim with its own applicability and limitation.

## Quality score

Score 100: authority 25, reproducibility 20, direct applicability 15, recency/version match 15, traceability 15, limitations/conflicts 10.

- 80–100: hard-rule candidate.
- 65–79: reviewed candidate.
- 50–64: prompt only.
- below 50: exploration pool.

## Daily increment

Fetch saved queries and whitelisted sources, canonicalize, deduplicate, triage, domain-review, conflict-check, then accept or quarantine. Report accepted, duplicate, rejected, pending, superseded, and coverage gaps. Freeze a monthly snapshot and rerun the gold eval set. Do not automate a schedule without explicit user approval.
