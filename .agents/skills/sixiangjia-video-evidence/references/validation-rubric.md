# Validation rubric

## Screenplay and advertising

- A screenplay exists before the shot list: protagonist, situation, desire, obstacle, conflict, emotional turn, decisive action, and resolution are identifiable.
- Scene actions dramatize the customer problem; they do not merely illustrate a narrated service checklist.
- Voiceover, shooting script, storyboard, prompts, and edit decisions trace back to the screenplay's dramatic beats.
- One customer problem, promise, CTA, and primary metric.
- Hook understandable within 2–3 seconds; brand cue within 5 seconds.
- Customer value precedes internal process.
- Every factual or professional claim has an evidence ID.

## First frame and raw clip

- Exact ratio; coherent hands, object geometry, material, light, wear, background, and continuity anchors.
- No fake logo/text/customer/store/case/result.
- Raw clip: ffprobe plus complete decode; extract all source-fps frames with PTS and issue ledger.
- Review at normal speed and frame level for action adherence, physics, object permanence, relation stability, causal compliance, flicker, and unwanted additions.

## Master

- Reproducible timeline/command; verified audio, subtitles, safe zones, duration, codec, resolution, and hashes.
- CTA and official brand/contact details are correct.
- Provenance and disclosure retained; raw sources preserved.

## Human and campaign evaluation

- Minimum five blind viewers only provides a smoke test, not ad-effectiveness proof.
- Record hook comprehension, perceived authenticity, trust, brand recall, CTA comprehension, and AI suspicion.
- Online tests preregister one primary metric and one changed variable. Track 2s/6s views, quartiles, average watch time, CTR, message starts, effective inquiries, and cost per effective inquiry.
- Without adequate sample/power, return `INCONCLUSIVE`.

## Automatic rejection

Reject untraceable cleaning claims; fake before/after; tool/object contact that contradicts the screenplay or shooting script; geometry or identity drift; generated brand text; cropped concealment of raw creative failure; AUP/provenance violation; or claims of effectiveness without campaign evidence.
