# 物件攝影配方庫 v1(#19)— 接在 style-master v2 之後使用

用法:生成提示詞 = phoneRealism(當日錨) + 本檔對應類別 3–5 條 + 主題指定的污損位置。
發布一律無品牌標誌;本庫詞彙描述「同級質感」,不指名品牌。

## 精品皮包(名牌包 $1500 / 特殊類 $2500)
- pebbled or smooth calfskin grain clearly visible, slight sheen only on high-touch points
- edge paint wear at corners, faint colour transfer on light leather
- brass or gold-tone hardware with micro-scratches and slight patina, stitching even and visible
- handle darkening from hand oils, gradual not dirty-looking
- structured body keeps its shape; slouch only where the topic says so

## 精品鞋(名牌鞋 $600)
- leather crease lines across the vamp at natural flex points
- outsole edge yellowing at the foxing line, tread dirt only in recesses
- suede nap direction visible with slight matting at toe
- lining wear at heel counter, faint sock print inside

## 一般球鞋/白鞋($250–350)
- midsole micro-creases and faint yellowing at seams
- lace fabric weave visible, slightly greyed
- mesh panels with trapped dust, canvas texture readable

## 皮衣/皮革衣物($700–2000)
- lambskin or cowhide grain with natural fold creases at elbows
- collar and cuff edge sheen from wear, faint mould spots only where topic says
- zipper tape slightly waved, hardware with patina

## 襯衫/西裝/制服
- cotton or wool weave readable at close distance
- collar ring inside the collar, cuff-fold grime line
- lapel roll natural, shoulder line soft not squared

## 寢具/羽絨(棉被 $350–500、羽絨羊毛被 $800)
- quilting channels with loft variation, down shifted where topic says
- fabric slub and slight pilling on the used side
- care label visible at a corner seam

## 絨毛娃娃
- fur strands individually resolvable, matting on hugged side
- glass or plastic eye with a single window catchlight
- seam lines slightly stressed

## 窗簾/地毯/布品
- hem dust gradient darker at the floor edge
- weave pattern readable, sun-fade band where topic says
- fold memory lines from hanging or storage

## 手入鏡(通用,取自船長 portrait-planning)
- one complete adult hand, five fingers, natural knuckle creases
- 右手做動作、左手穩定物體;接觸點與受力寫清楚
- 動作像正在發生:布剛壓下、拉鍊拉到一半、標籤剛翻開
