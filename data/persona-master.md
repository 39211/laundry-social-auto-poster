# 私享家職人人設錨 v1(師傅出鏡用,船長身份錨方法)

## 身份錨(每次生成逐字使用,鎖同一張臉)
Taiwanese man in his early 40s, oval face with slightly squared jaw, short neat black hair with a few grey strands at the temples, monolid eyes with fine smile lines at the corners, medium-tan working-hands skin tone, natural skin texture with visible pores, slight facial asymmetry, calm focused expression with a faint craftsman's pride
## 服裝錨
dark navy canvas work apron over a plain light-grey short-sleeve tee, sleeves pushed to forearms, clean but visibly used apron with faint water marks
## 場景錨
同 style-master v2(粉紅切割墊+白溝槽牆+背景錨輪替)
## 負面約束
no idol-like beauty, no over-retouched skin, no plastic look, not younger than 38, no readable text or logos
## 用法
師傅只在「判斷/檢查」鏡頭出現(看物件、指位置、手上有工具),不看鏡頭擺拍;每支影片至多 1-2 鏡,物件仍是主角。
