# 私享家風格母版 v1(2026-08-10 深夜)

唯一正本。所有圖片/影片提示詞必須以此為底,逐字取用「場景 DNA + 攝影規格 + 材質詞彙 + 負面約束」。
方法論來自船長AI視界(風格母版+真人感變數+質感詞彙),場景細節來自私享家 Google Maps 實照。

## 場景 DNA(三選一,每張圖鎖定一個)

### A. 檢查櫃台(預設)
white slat-wall panels behind, a light counter with a PINK cutting mat, garment conveyor with plastic-covered clothes softly blurred in the background, retail shelves with care products on the left, everyday Taiwanese laundry shop clutter

### B. 鞋牆展示
rows of cleaned sneakers on reflective glass shelves against white slat-wall, mixed shoe styles visible, shallow reflections on the glass

### C. 門口(僅街景主題用)
green corrugated-metal facade with white Chinese sign lettering and a red vertical sign panel, an old arched doorway, a leafy tree at frame edge, Taichung street daylight

## 攝影規格(每張都要,逐字放)
shot on a phone, 35mm documentary perspective, f/2.8 feel with the object sharp and the background still recognizable, soft mixed light from the storefront window and fluorescent ceiling, gentle shadow falloff, natural highlight roll-off, Kodak Portra 400 inspired warm tone, subtle film grain, slight handheld framing imperfection

## 物件材質詞彙(按物件家族取 3–5 個)

- 皮鞋/皮包: pebbled or smooth leather grain visible, creases at flex points, edge-paint wear on corners, brass hardware with slight patina, contact shadow under the object
- 球鞋/白鞋: midsole texture with faint yellowing at seams, lace fabric weave, suede nap direction visible, sole tread dirt in recesses
- 襯衫/西裝: cotton or wool weave visible, natural folds at elbows, collar-ring detail inside the collar, stitch lines on lapels
- 寢具/布品: quilting lines, fabric slub texture, slight pilling where used, soft volume not flat
- 絨毛娃娃: fur strands individually visible with matting on worn side, seam lines, glass-eye catchlight
- 手(出現時): natural skin texture with visible knuckle creases, one hand only doing one clear action, no second ambiguous hand

## 負面約束(每張放,逐字)
not editorial, not studio lighting, no readable brand logos or text, no plastic or waxy surfaces, no showroom-new look unless the topic is the after state, no artificial background blur, no oversaturated colors

## 組裝規則(給生成者)
1. 場景選 A/B/C 一個,逐字放場景 DNA
2. 攝影規格整段逐字放
3. 材質詞按物件家族挑 3–5 個
4. 主體狀態要呼應主題(dirty/mid-cleaning/cleaned),污損寫在具體位置
5. 負面約束整段收尾
6. 全文讀起來要像攝影企劃,不是關鍵字堆疊
