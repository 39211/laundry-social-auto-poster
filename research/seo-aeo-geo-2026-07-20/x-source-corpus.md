# X source corpus: SEO / AEO / GEO / Local AI Search

Collected 2026-07-20 with the read-only Grok X search workflow. Search window: 2025-07-20 through 2026-07-20. Three focused sweeps covered (A) AI search/GEO measurement, (B) local SEO/GBP/Maps, and (C) crawl/index/schema/IndexNow/measurement. The sweeps returned 155 candidates; mechanical URL de-duplication leaves **153 unique X status URLs**.

This is a discovery corpus, not a vote and not proof that every claim is true. The associated audit gives official sources priority, separates platform announcements and measured experiments from opinions, and rejects ranking guarantees. Some URLs may appear in more than one topical sweep; the unique-count check is recorded in the audit.

## Interpretation rule

- Official platform documentation and first-party product definitions outrank X commentary.
- Server-log experiments are evidence about fetching, not automatically about indexing, ranking, citation, traffic, or bookings.
- Ranking guarantees, one-number AI visibility claims, and unsupported causal claims are excluded from implementation decisions.
